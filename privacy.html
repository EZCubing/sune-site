Privacy policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Sune is a Rubik's cube timer for iPhone where you can share solves with friends. This page says what Sune stores, why, and how to have it deleted.

What Sune stores

  • Your sign-in. When you sign in with Apple or Google, Sune receives an account identifier and your email address, and your name if you choose to share it.
  • Your profile. Your username, display name, profile photo, country, the cubes you main, and any YouTube, Instagram or TikTok handles you add.
  • Your solves. Each solve's time, penalty, scramble, event and date, and whether you shared it.
  • Your friends. Who you've added, and the fire you give and get on shared solves.
  • A notification token. If you allow notifications, a token that lets Sune tell you when someone fires your solve.

Who can see it

  • Anyone signed in to Sune can see your username, name, photo, country, mains and socials, so friends can find you.
  • Only your accepted friends see the solves you share, your records and your averages. Solves you don't share are visible only to you.
  • Your email is never shown to other people.

How it's used

Only to run Sune: signing you in, showing your profile and solves, your friends' feed, and notifications. Sune has no ads, does not sell or share your data, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Where it's kept

Sune's data is stored with Supabase on servers in the United States. Notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple. Sign-in is handled by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Email ezekieljaramillo02@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask to delete your account. Your profile, photo, solves, friends and notification tokens will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Children

Sune is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
